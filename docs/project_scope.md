# TRIP Project Scope

## Project Name

TRIP — Tanzania Readmission Intelligence Platform

## Primary Objective

To investigate whether routinely available patient and hospital information can be used to identify patients at elevated risk of hospital readmission.

## Initial Prediction Task

Predict hospital readmission within 30 days following discharge.

## Initial Development Dataset

UCI Diabetes 130-US Hospitals Dataset.

This dataset will be used as a baseline research dataset for model development and methodological training.

The resulting model should not be interpreted as a clinically validated Tanzanian model.

## Long-Term Direction

Future stages may include:

- MIMIC-IV analysis
- Tanzania health-system contextual data
- local hospital partnerships
- external validation
- local model recalibration
- Tanzania-specific clinical implementation research

## Core Research Areas

1. Health data analysis
2. Biostatistics
3. Machine learning
4. Model validation
5. Explainable AI
6. Fairness analysis
7. Clinical decision support
8. Population health analytics
9. Health information systems
10. Tanzania digital health adaptation

## Intended Users

Potential future users may include:

- clinicians
- hospital administrators
- researchers
- public-health analysts
- healthcare planners

## Current Limitation

TRIP is currently a research prototype and must not be treated as a clinical diagnostic or treatment system.
